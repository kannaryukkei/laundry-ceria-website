## 3. Audience discovery

### Segment A — Boarding-house residents

**Source fact:** Boarding-house residents are one of the stated primary customer groups (NOTE_LINE [5]).

**Working needs hypotheses (not confirmed customer research):**
- Need to compare service options and prices before deciding.
- May value a clear explanation of drop-off, weighing, collection, and completion.
- May find pickup/delivery useful when they cannot visit the shop.

### Segment B — Young families

**Source fact:** Young families around the shop are another stated primary customer group (NOTE_LINE [5]).

**Working needs hypotheses (not confirmed customer research):**
- May need straightforward service and pricing information for recurring household laundry.
- May find the bed-cover and blanket services relevant.
- May value the pickup/delivery option for heavier orders.

### Audience boundary

The fixture does not provide customer interviews, age ranges, income levels, exact neighborhoods, purchase frequency, or validated motivations. Do not present the hypotheses above as customer-proven facts or invent demographic personas.

## 4. Customer questions the website should answer

These are strategy-derived information needs, not quotations from customers:

1. What laundry services are available?
2. How much does each service cost?
3. How long does regular and express laundry take?
4. What are the express cutoff time and surcharge?
5. Is pickup/delivery available, what is its radius, and what does it cost?
6. Where is the shop and when is it open?
7. What happens after the customer hands over the laundry?
8. How can a customer visit or contact the business?

## 5. Positioning strategy

### Recommended positioning statement — internal strategy, not final website copy

Laundry Ceria is a neighborhood laundry service for boarding-house residents and young families around its shop, offering clearly itemized laundry options and prices, a customer-visible weighing and recording process, and a defined pickup/delivery option within a 2 km radius.

### Value proposition pillars

| Pillar | Source-backed support | Communication guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Clear service choices and prices | Service list and prices are provided in NOTE_LINE [3], [4], [23], [24] | Display the supplied prices exactly; do not invent minimum charges or extra fees |
| Clear handover process | Weighing in front of the customer and per-customer recording, NOTE_LINE [7] | Describe the process; do not turn the owner's no-mix-up claim into a verified guarantee |
| Flexible turnaround | Regular service is usually two days; express terms are specified, NOTE_LINE [10], [25] | Preserve “usually” for regular timing and state the express cutoff and surcharge accurately |
| Neighborhood convenience | Pickup/delivery radius and fees are specified, NOTE_LINE [27] | Do not imply service outside the 2 km radius or change the fee threshold |
| Practical completion detail | Free plastic wrapping, NOTE_LINE [29] | State only that completed orders are wrapped in plastic for free |

### Claim control

The owner claims that laundry does not get mixed up because items are weighed and recorded per customer (NOTE_LINE [16], supported as a process by NOTE_LINE [7]). This is an owner claim, not independently verified evidence. The website may describe weighing and recording, but must not use absolute claims such as “guaranteed never mixed up” or “100% safe.”

## 6. Website goal and conversion strategy

### Main website goal

Help prospective customers understand Laundry Ceria's services, prices, operating details, and ordering options so they can choose to visit the shop or contact the business.

### Conversion paths

| Path | Intended action | Current readiness | Implementation rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk-in | Visit the shop | Address is present in the fixture but is fictional | Show only within a clearly marked training preview; do not create real navigation/map directions or imply the address is real |
| WhatsApp | Contact the business | NOT_CONFIGURED — number missing | Do not generate a `wa.me` URL, invent a phone number, or present a working WhatsApp action. Show a non-functional “WhatsApp belum dikonfigurasi” preview state if a visual CTA is needed |

The strategic conversion model is therefore dual-path, but neither path should be represented as production-ready in this fictional preview. The walk-in address itself is also fictional.

## 7. Recommended website approach

**Recommendation:** A focused, responsive, single-page local-service website.

**Why:** The stated goal is narrow—introduce services and explain how to order—and the source provides a compact set of information that can be understood on one page. This is a studio recommendation, not a source fact.

### Information priorities

1. Business and service summary.
2. Service categories and exact prices.
3. Regular and express turnaround details.
4. Pickup/delivery coverage and fees.
5. How the weighing, recording, and washing process works.
6. Opening hours and fictional preview address.
7. Walk-in and WhatsApp conversion states, with unavailable actions clearly identified.

## 8. Tone and brand direction

**Recommended tone:** friendly, straightforward, practical, and clear.

**Writing principles:**
- Use everyday Bahasa Indonesia.
- Put service names, prices, time commitments, and conditions in plain language.
- Avoid exaggerated superlatives and unsupported trust claims.
- Keep instructions short and actionable.

No logo or formal brand guide is supplied (NOTE_LINE [19], [31]). A temporary text-based wordmark may be used in the preview, clearly treated as a design placeholder rather than an existing logo.

## 9. Project scope

### In scope for the preview website

- Responsive single-page presentation.
- Service and price information based strictly on the fixture.
- Regular and express service explanation.
- Pickup/delivery rules.
- Process explanation using the documented handover workflow.
- Operating hours and fictional address, visibly labelled as demo data.
- Non-functional WhatsApp CTA state, visibly marked as not configured.
- Clear preview-only notice and safeguards against accidental publication.

### Out of scope unless separately specified

- Online checkout or payment collection.
- Live order status, customer accounts, or order tracking.
- Automated WhatsApp messaging or a real WhatsApp integration.
- Booking engine or order-management dashboard.
- Claims, testimonials, ratings, awards, or customer statistics.
- Real map navigation to the fictional address.
- Use of the three described photos before actual files and written permission are supplied.
