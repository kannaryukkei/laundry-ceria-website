## 1. Authority rule

AI and coding tools may inspect, draft, implement previously authorized work and prepare evidence. They cannot confirm client facts, grant asset rights, approve commercial scope, authorize spending, authorize publication or release, or transfer ownership.

A gate is passed only when its named authority, exact subject/version, explicit decision and durable evidence are recorded. A successful build, a draft PR, a completed handoff document, silence or an AI recommendation is not an approval.
