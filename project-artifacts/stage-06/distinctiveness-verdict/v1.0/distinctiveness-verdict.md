# Stage 6 — Distinctiveness Verdict

**Selected fingerprint:** LC-FINGERPRINT-001 v1.0 — Fresh & Orderly  
**Verdict:** COMPETENT BUT GENERIC — distinctiveness beyond this project is NOT YET PROVEN.

## Visual review findings

The rendered site has a coherent visual system:
- Deep teal, ink, warm paper, mint and restrained amber are applied consistently.
- Hero hierarchy, pricing cards, timing panels and service-information blocks are easy to scan.
- Abstract folded-fabric artwork is decorative rather than fake business photography.
- Mobile and desktop compositions preserve the same design language.
- The preview notice and unavailable-contact state are visible and understandable.

The result is polished and internally consistent for a one-page local service site. However, its composition relies on familiar service-site patterns: a split hero, symmetric information cards, feature/price blocks, numbered process rows and paired contact cards. Those patterns make it competent but not strongly ownable.

## Comparison limitation

The earlier issue ledger explicitly deferred comparison against at least two recent relevant Studio projects. This E2E branch does not contain two verified comparator project fingerprints/screenshots, and no such comparison was completed during this cycle. No claim of portfolio-level originality is therefore made.

## Decision

- Keep the selected fingerprint for the Laundry Ceria synthetic fixture.
- Do not promote it as a proven Studio-wide signature.
- Future design-system learning should compare at least two separate, real Studio project fingerprints and document which reusable patterns are shared versus project-specific.
- Do not add decorative novelty that harms price readability, source accuracy or preview disclosure merely to appear distinctive.

**System-learning status:** NOT YET PROVEN beyond this fixture.
