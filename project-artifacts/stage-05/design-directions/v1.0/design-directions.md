# Stage 5 — Two Materially Different Design Directions

**Classification:** SYNTHETIC / DEVELOPMENT ONLY  
**Input:** WRP-LC-E2E-001 v1.0; Stage 3 design direction; existing code snapshot  
**No logo or business photography is approved.**

## Direction A — Fresh & Orderly

- **Concept:** calm, clear neighborhood-service presentation; freshness and operational order.
- **Palette:** deep teal, ink blue, warm paper, mint, restrained amber.
- **Typography:** geometric display headings paired with clean sans-serif body.
- **Layout:** spacious single-page editorial grid, strong section hierarchy, price tables and paired cards.
- **Imagery:** abstract folded-fabric shapes only, explicitly decorative.
- **Interaction:** simple anchors, clear status labels, neutral disabled states.
- **Strength:** aligns with supplied Stage 3 specification and current implementation; makes prices, hours and conditions easy to scan.
- **Risk:** dark areas and small eyebrow labels need rendered contrast and responsive checks.

## Direction B — Warm Utility

- **Concept:** practical neighborhood service board, closer to a printed counter menu.
- **Palette:** warm sand, clay/orange, dark brown/charcoal, cream and muted olive.
- **Typography:** compact humanist sans-serif with slab-like display accents.
- **Layout:** denser modular information blocks, horizontal service index, stronger utilitarian labels, less whitespace.
- **Imagery:** typographic labels and simple line diagrams; no photographic assumptions.
- **Interaction:** filter-like tabs would be unnecessary; all content remains on one page.
- **Strength:** strongly prioritizes price and operating detail; could feel approachable and practical.
- **Risk:** denser cards and warm accent backgrounds may reduce hierarchy clarity at narrow widths; would require new contrast and responsive validation.

## Material differences

The options differ in palette, typographic character, density, card geometry, content rhythm and overall emotional tone. They are not minor color variations of the same layout.

## Decision status

Both are internal design proposals. No client preference exists in the fictional fixture. Selection must be recorded as a studio development choice, not client preference.
