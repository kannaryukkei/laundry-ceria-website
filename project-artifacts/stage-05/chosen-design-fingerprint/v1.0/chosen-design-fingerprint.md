# Stage 5 — Chosen Design Fingerprint

**Selected direction:** A — Fresh & Orderly  
**Fingerprint ID:** LC-FINGERPRINT-001 v1.0  
**Selection authority:** Studio development operator; no client design approval exists.

## Distinctive system

- Calm, clean and organized one-page composition.
- Deep teal as the primary interaction color.
- Ink blue headings and body text.
- Warm off-white page canvas with white information cards.
- Soft mint used for supporting panels and icon surfaces.
- Restrained amber accents, always paired with dark text.
- Express price/time conditions and pickup thresholds are presented as separate, legible facts.
- Typography uses Plus Jakarta Sans for display and Inter for body, with system fallbacks.
- Abstract folded-fabric artwork is explicitly decorative.
- Rounded cards and consistent spacing establish approachable service presentation.
- Preview notice is a persistent first-class element and repeated in the footer.
- WhatsApp is a neutral disabled NOT_CONFIGURED state.
- Fictional address remains visibly labelled and non-clickable.

## Anti-template constraints

- Do not use a generic luxury-services hero.
- Do not introduce fake photography, logo or social proof.
- Do not overuse gradients, glassmorphism, neon, oversized shadows or animation.
- Do not reduce important price, hour, notice or disabled-state text to micro typography.
- Keep service details and thresholds more prominent than decorative art.

## Implementation alignment

The current repository implementation uses this visual direction. The chosen fingerprint records a synthetic design choice, not a retrospective client approval of the code.
