## Summary

The fixture describes a small kiloan laundry operating from a shop-house for three years. The fictional owner is supported by one employee. Named services are cuci lipat, cuci setrika and setrika saja, plus bed cover and selimut by piece. The stated audience is boarding-house residents and young families near the shop. Most customers bring items to the shop.

The described workflow is: receive laundry, weigh it in front of the customer, record it per customer, wash it, dry it, fold or iron according to the selected service, then notify the customer through WhatsApp. Completed laundry is wrapped in plastic at no additional charge.
