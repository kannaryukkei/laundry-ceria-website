## Segment A — Boarding-house residents

**Source evidence:** “penghuni kos” around the shop (NOTE_LINE [5]).

**Possible job to be done:** Understand which laundry service is suitable, compare the stated price, and know when items may be ready.

**Needs hypotheses:**
- Quickly compare cuci lipat, cuci setrika and setrika saja.
- Understand kiloan price units and typical turnaround.
- Know the exact express cutoff, completion time and surcharge.
- Understand pickup/delivery boundary and fees.

**Evidence gaps:** No age, income, schedule, purchase frequency, interview quote, access needs, or validated barrier data.

## Segment B — Young families

**Source evidence:** “keluarga muda” around the shop (NOTE_LINE [5]).

**Possible job to be done:** Understand laundry options for routine household items and heavier individual pieces.

**Needs hypotheses:**
- Compare kiloan and piece-based services.
- Identify bed-cover and blanket prices.
- Understand whether pickup/delivery could apply to their order weight.
- See opening hours and handover process.

**Evidence gaps:** No household size, service frequency, preference, location boundary, or validated customer research.

## Shared visitor questions

1. What services are available?
2. What does each service cost and what is the charging unit?
3. How long does regular laundry usually take?
4. What are the express cutoff, completion time and surcharge?
5. Is pickup/delivery available, within what radius and at what fee?
6. What is the handover process?
7. What hours and address are shown in the fixture?
8. What contact or visit action is actually available?

## Accessibility and trust needs

These are design requirements, not discovered preferences:
- Prices, units, hours and express terms should be plain and scannable.
- The 5 kg threshold must be unambiguous: exactly 5 kg is in the Rp5.000 bracket.
- Fictional address and NOT_CONFIGURED WhatsApp must be clearly disclosed.
- A visible preview notice must prevent the training data being mistaken for a live business.
- Keyboard navigation, readable type and responsive layout should be checked.

## Segment prioritization

Prioritize both source-named groups equally because the fixture provides no evidence to rank them. Do not create personas or demographic claims beyond the supplied group labels.
