# Stage 3 — Buying Journey

**Classification:** SYNTHETIC / DEVELOPMENT ONLY  
**Journey type:** Planned informational journeys; no observed analytics or validated user research.

## Journey A — Compare laundry options

1. Visitor lands on the preview and reads the persistent demo notice.
2. Scans the hero introduction.
3. Opens Services & Prices.
4. Compares kiloan and piece-based options, prices and units.
5. Reads regular and express time details.
6. Leaves with an understanding of the fixture offer, not a real-world transaction.

**Questions:** Q-0001, Q-0002, Q-0003.  
**Success:** the visitor can identify the five listed options and exact units without mistaking prices for a live offer.

## Journey B — Understand pickup/delivery

1. Visitor navigates to Pickup & Delivery.
2. Reads 2 km radius.
3. Compares >5 kg free with ≤5 kg Rp5.000.
4. Understands that no scheduling or booking action is available.

**Question:** Q-0005.  
**Success:** exact threshold is clear; exactly 5 kg appears in paid bracket; no unsupported coverage or booking promise.

## Journey C — Understand process and trust

1. Visitor opens How It Works.
2. Reads weighing in front of the customer and per-customer recording.
3. Reads wash/dry/finish and notification description.
4. Sees free plastic wrapping detail.
5. Does not interpret the process as a zero-mix-up guarantee or live WhatsApp integration.

**Questions:** Q-0004, Q-0007.  
**Success:** the process is understood; unsupported guarantees are absent.

## Journey D — Consider contacting or visiting

1. Visitor reads hours and address.
2. Sees adjacent fictional-address disclosure.
3. Navigates to Contact.
4. Sees WhatsApp NOT_CONFIGURED and disabled.
5. Does not receive a fake map, phone number, link or QR.

**Questions:** Q-0006, Q-0008.  
**Success:** preview status and unavailable conversion actions are unmistakable.

## Friction and failure handling

- No real-world action should execute from the preview.
- Anchor navigation must work without JavaScript-dependent external links.
- The disabled WhatsApp card should not be focusable or expose a fake link.
- The address is informational demo text only.
- The site must preserve the notice at narrow viewports and zoomed layouts.
